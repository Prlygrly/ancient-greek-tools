Typing help ▼
Letters: most keys are phonetic (a→α, b→β …). Special: j→η, w→ω, q/th→θ, f/ph→φ, c/kh→χ, y/ps→ψ, x→ξ.
Breathing: h where you hear it (ho→ὁ, hoi→οἱ); press h again for smooth. Accents: / or ' acute, \ grave, = or ~ circumflex, | iota subscript, : diaeresis — typed after the vowel.
Question mark: ? → ;  ·  final sigma is automatic  ·  full reference in the keyboard tool.
